Limitations of the study

This study is limited by the number and demographic diversity of available donors, as well as inherent constraints associated with postmortem human tissue. Although our multimodal spatial framework provides a comprehensive baseline, additional orthogonal validations such as immunohistochemistry, in situ sequencing, and in vivo electrophysiological or connectivity assays would further strengthen cell-type and spatial community assignments. Future work should extend this approach to a broader range of ages and demographic backgrounds and apply this methodology to comparative analyses of neurological and psychiatric disorders in human tissue.
